## Evidence interpretation

The live promotion itself is verified. However, the following remain unpublished in the official public material found:

1. whether a RadioArena promo code credits sports-only bonus balance or general account value;
2. whether `Lotereya` / Super Keno accepts that balance;
3. wagering/turnover requirement;
4. minimum odds or coupon structure if sports-only;
5. expiry;
6. whether winnings generated from the promo are withdrawable and under what conversion rule;
7. number of codes awarded per show / probability of winning.

The `#Futbol` tag is useful evidence that the campaign is sports-oriented, but it is not strong enough to prove sports-only eligibility. Conversely, Misli's platform-wide inclusion of `Lotereya` is not proof that the code can buy lottery tickets.

## Rejected evidence

Public third-party/affiliate-style pages claim generic Misli lottery promo codes and bonus rules. They are not official Misli/Azerlotereya sources; at least one explicitly disclaims affiliation. Their claimed codes, wagering rules and lottery eligibility are therefore **not used** in the EV ledger.

## Conditional EV remains unchanged

`10 * 0.5918070335 = 5.918070335 AZN`

Because the promo itself costs the winner zero personal stake, that is positive expected incremental cash value per awarded code. It is not yet an actionable Super-Keno edge because eligibility is unresolved and award probability is unknown.
