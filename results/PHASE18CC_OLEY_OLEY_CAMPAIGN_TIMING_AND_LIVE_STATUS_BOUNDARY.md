# Phase 18CC — Oley Oley campaign timing and live-status boundary

Date: 2026-08-29

## Question

Can the Oley Oley overlay be treated as a currently executable Super Keno modifier, and can its weekly denominator be recovered from public campaign chronology?

## New evidence

1. Misli's official YouTube launch post dated **2026-06-09** states that, after joining the campaign, every **5 AZN** in sports betting earns **1 chance**, while every **5 AZN** in the other games — explicitly including **Virtual İdman, ePoz-Qazan, Lotereya** — earns **2 chances**. The launch inventory is stated as **2 Changan UNI-Z + 18 iPhone 17 Pro + 30 PlayStation 5 + 60,000 AZN bonus**.

2. A Misli operator-channel Telegram post for the **third week** repeats the same earning rule and lists the remaining campaign inventory as **1 Changan UNI-Z + 9 iPhone 17 Pro + 15 PlayStation 5 + 30,000 AZN bonus**. This is exactly one-half of the launch non-car inventory and one of two cars, consistent with a six-week campaign with three weeks already completed.

3. A 2026-07-17 article reproducing the Misli campaign copy states that the **first car owner had already been determined** and that the **second car owner would be determined in the following week's draw**. The same article again repeats the `Lotereya = 2 chances per 5 AZN` rule.

## Inference

The public chronology is internally consistent with a campaign launched 2026-06-09 and progressing through weekly draws into July. By 2026-07-17, only the second car remained to be awarded and it was scheduled for the following week's draw.

This is strong enough to downgrade Oley Oley from `live modifier` to **`historically verified / current live status unproven`** at the 2026-08-29 snapshot. We should not count it as executable today unless a materially new current account UI, Misli campaign page, support reply, or operator post proves that the campaign remains active or has been renewed.

The public chronology does **not** reveal the weekly eligible-chance denominator `T_week`. Winning-number posts alone should not be used to infer `T_week` unless their numbering semantics are proven.

## EV consequence

The theoretical threshold remains useful for future renewals or analogous campaigns:

- Super Keno base cash EV ratio = `0.5918070335`.
- For 5 AZN stake, missing EV to break even = `5 * (1 - 0.5918070335) = 2.0409648325 AZN`.
- Lotereya earns 2 promo chances per 5 AZN, so required expected value per promo chance = `1.02048241625 AZN`.
- Bonus-only weekly prize mass of 10,000 AZN would alone cover the gap if `T_week < 10,000 / 1.02048241625 ≈ 9,799` chances, before applying any bonus withdrawal/turnover haircut.
