## Current governing rule located

Current published `Lotereyaların təşkili və keçirilməsi Qaydası`, decision №1951100016 dated 11.03.2019, states in §2.2.5 that lottery conditions must include:

- ticket/variant price;
- ticket sample;
- **number and numbers of lottery tickets**;
- acquisition rules;

with one explicit exception: the ticket quantity/numbers requirement does not apply to lotteries where the prize-fund amount is determined as a function of sold-ticket amount.

The same rule states in §2.4 that the organizer must provide the lottery conditions to a participant on request, and in §2.5 that the organizer must announce the lottery conditions through mass media after registration and **before ticket sales begin**.

Source: Central Bank publication of decision №1951100016, current page crawled 2026-08-28.

## Why this matters for `1001 Sevinc`

The official `1001 Sevinc` page continues to identify the product as registration **316 / 12.05.2025** and describes prize-category draws scheduled in advance, with an earlier-draw mechanism if tickets sell out early. Current product behaviour therefore remains consistent with a finite released pool per prize-category draw.

But the legal exception above must be handled explicitly. We should not claim that registration 316 necessarily contains a ticket count until we establish that `1001 Sevinc` is **not** classified under the sold-dependent prize-fund exception.

### Working classification

`1001 Sevinc` appears economically to use a fixed non-cash prize for each category/draw rather than a prize whose value scales with ticket sales. This makes §2.2.5's ordinary ticket-count requirement the stronger working interpretation, but this is an inference until the registered conditions themselves are obtained.
